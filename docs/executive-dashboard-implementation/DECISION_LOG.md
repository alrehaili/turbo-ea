### 2.1 Implementation details settled under D-19, D-21, D-26 and D-17a (2026-10-25)

Narrow points not spelled out in the approvals, decided in their spirit and listed here for review:

| # | Detail | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| I-1 | Periods a KPI can be measured for | Fiscal year and fiscal quarter, the keys the executive overview already uses (`FY2027`, `FY2027-Q2`). Monthly periods are not supported yet. |
| I-2 | Period-end value of a system-calculated KPI | The **last daily observation inside the period**, recorded as a `submitted` period measurement after the period closes, with its observation date. It still needs verification. No value is recomputed after the fact. |
| I-3 | Recorder of a system value | The system: any holder of `kpis.verify` may verify it. A person who records or corrects a measurement can never verify that row. |
| I-4 | KPI card `currentValue` / `lastMeasuredDate` | Not written from the measurement history (one writer per field); the card's History tab shows each measurement event. Syncing them is left open. |
| I-5 | Who reads a KPI's history | Anyone who may read the KPI card (`CardReadScope`, hidden types excluded). History of system-calculated metrics with no card (the four legacy series) keeps the dashboard's existing rule. |
| I-6 | Definition approved | The KPI card's approval status is Approved; this is not target ratification. |
